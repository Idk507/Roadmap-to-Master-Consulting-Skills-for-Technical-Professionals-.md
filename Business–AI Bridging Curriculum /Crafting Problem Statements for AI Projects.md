# 📌 Crafting Problem Statements for AI Projects

---

## 1️⃣ Structure of a Business Problem Statement

A strong **business problem statement** answers 4 questions:

1. **Who** – Which business unit, customer segment, or process is impacted?
2. **What** – What is the issue or challenge?
3. **Why** – Why does it matter (business pain / strategic goal)?
4. **Impact** – What is the measurable cost or opportunity size?

🔑 **Formula:**

> “$Who$ is experiencing $What$ problem, which matters because $Why$, leading to $Impact$.”

---

### ✅ Example

> **Problem Statement:**
> “The retail bank’s customer service team is facing high call volumes, leading to long wait times. This matters because customer satisfaction scores have dropped by 12% in the last quarter, which directly impacts customer retention and brand trust.”

---

## 2️⃣ Translating Business Challenges into Data/AI Questions

Once the business problem is clear, the consultant bridges it to AI by asking:

👉 **What data do we need?**
👉 **What AI/ML/GenAI technique could help?**
👉 **What’s the output the business can use?**

---

### ✅ Example

**Business Problem:**
“Customer churn has increased by 15% over the last 6 months, costing \$20M annually.”

**AI Translation:**

* Data: Historical customer transactions, complaints, usage frequency, demographics
* AI Question: *“Which customers are most likely to churn in the next 3 months?”*
* Solution: Build a **churn prediction model** + design **personalized retention offers** (discounts, upgrades).
* Business Outcome: Reduce churn by 5%, saving \~\$7M annually.

---

## 3️⃣ Example Walkthrough (End-to-End Real Scenario)

### Scenario: **E-commerce Company – Returns Problem**

**Step 1 – Business Problem Statement (Who, What, Why, Impact):**

* **Who:** E-commerce operations team
* **What:** High return rates (25% of orders returned)
* **Why:** This increases logistics cost and reduces margins
* **Impact:** Estimated \$50M annual loss due to shipping + refund handling

**Step 2 – AI Translation:**

* **Data Needed:** Product attributes, customer profiles, purchase history, return reasons
* **AI Question:** *“Can we predict which orders are most likely to be returned?”*
* **Solution:** Build ML model for return prediction + GenAI chatbot for pre-purchase Q\&A (size guidance, reviews summary)
* **Business Outcome:** Reduce return rate from 25% → 20%, saving \~\$10M annually.

## 4️⃣ Practice: 5 AI Problem Statements Across Industries

Here are **five practice examples** to internalize the skill:

---

### 1. **Finance (Credit Risk)**

* **Problem Statement:**
  “The bank is facing higher loan defaults (up 8% YoY). This matters because it increases credit risk exposure, costing \$30M annually in bad loans.”
* **AI Translation:**
  *“Build a credit risk scoring model that predicts likelihood of default at the time of loan application, so the bank can adjust approval criteria and reduce risk.”*

---

### 2. **Retail (Personalization)**

* **Problem Statement:**
  “The retailer’s online conversion rate is only 2%, while competitors achieve 5%. This matters because low conversion directly reduces revenue growth.”
* **AI Translation:**
  *“Use recommendation models and GenAI-driven personalized product descriptions to improve conversion rates and boost sales.”*

---

### 3. **Healthcare (Patient Readmission)**

* **Problem Statement:**
  “A hospital is experiencing a 20% readmission rate within 30 days of discharge. This matters because it leads to \$15M in penalty costs under healthcare regulations.”
* **AI Translation:**
  *“Build a predictive model to identify high-risk patients at discharge, and recommend tailored follow-up care plans to reduce readmissions.”*

---

### 4. **Insurance (Claims Fraud)**

* **Problem Statement:**
  “The insurance company is losing \$50M annually due to fraudulent claims slipping through manual reviews. This impacts profitability and compliance.”
* **AI Translation:**
  *“Use anomaly detection models + GenAI for claims text analysis to flag potentially fraudulent claims for investigation.”*

---

### 5. **Telecom (Customer Support)**

* **Problem Statement:**
  “The telecom provider’s customer support call wait times average 15 minutes. This matters because customer satisfaction dropped by 10 points, increasing churn risk.”
* **AI Translation:**
  *“Deploy a GenAI-driven customer support assistant to handle FAQs and transactional queries, reducing call center load and improving satisfaction scores.”*

---

## 5️⃣ Consultant’s Checklist

When writing a problem statement, check:

✔ Did I **avoid technical jargon** in the business problem?
✔ Did I show **why it matters in \$ or % terms**?
✔ Did I link the problem to a **clear AI question and solution**?
✔ Did I emphasize the **business outcome, not the model**?

---

# 🎯 Key Takeaway

* **Business leaders speak in revenue, cost, risk, and customer terms.**
* **AI teams speak in data, models, and accuracy.**
* Your job as a consultant: **bridge the two.**

👉 If you can consistently turn:
“X business pain” → “AI question” → “Business outcome with ROI”
you’ll **stand out as a Business–AI translator**.
